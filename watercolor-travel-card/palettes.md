# Watercolor Travel Card Palettes

Use these watercolor palettes to translate the source poster's colors into transparent washes while keeping the brand's recognizable color signal. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Warm Ivory Sea

Best for coastal, travel, fish, and calming campaigns.

- paper: warm ivory cold-pressed
- wash: aqua, powder blue, warm cream
- accents: dust peach, sand, soft teal
- detail: broken linework, pigment bloom, paper highlights

## Moss And Rice

Best for tea, green products, natural, and wellness campaigns.

- paper: warm ivory cotton
- wash: sage green, moss, warm oat
- accents: apricot, cream, muted plum
- detail: water marks, settling pigment, light unfinished edges

## Aged Rose

Best for lifestyle, gift, and gentle member offers.

- paper: warm ivory board
- wash: dusty rose, cream, pale brown
- accents: muted coral, soft taupe, warm gray
- detail: slight overrun, granulation, fine pen accents

## Archive Blue

Best for premium, tech-adjacent, and formal service campaigns.

- paper: warm ivory cold-press
- wash: slate blue, warm gray, cream
- accents: restrained gold, dusty lavender
- detail: sparse washes, editorial archive column, fine rule

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal, expressed as watercolor.
- Keep the palette soft and limited; the archive column stays restrained.
- Preserve abundant paper white (warm ivory) for readability.
- Add a fine pen-line accent and reserved highlights for depth.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve a calmer, lower-detail paper zone behind it.
