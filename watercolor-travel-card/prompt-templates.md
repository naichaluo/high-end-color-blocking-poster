# Prompt Templates

Use these templates as starting points. Replace bracketed text with the current source content. Remove any line that does not apply.

## Existing Poster To Watercolor Travel Card

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: vertical commercial poster (3:4)
Primary request: Redraw the provided source poster as a perforated watercolor travel-card poster.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and content reference.
Source-of-truth content: preserve the exact brand, headline, offer amount, product labels, QR position, service text, and overall information hierarchy from Image 1.
Style/medium: transparent watercolor on warm ivory cold-pressed paper, fine pen lines, pigment bloom and settling, broken linework, controlled washes, reserved paper highlights, lightly unfinished edges; left 68-75% scene, right 25-32% restrained travel archive column separated by a row of uniform circular perforations.
Composition/framing: keep the source poster's main layout logic: top brand and headline, central product scene, lower offer module, QR/action area, bottom service/legal line.
Scene/backdrop: [describe one central watercolor subject from the source; let the environment fade into the page].
Lighting/mood: quiet, transparent, gentle, restrained, independent travel-journal mood.
Color palette: derive a soft limited watercolor palette from the source; preserve signature subject colors and overall temperature; keep warm ivory paper abundant.
Text handling: leave quiet areas for exact text overlays; do not invent or alter business copy.
Constraints: do not mix in any content from previous projects or other reference images; do not change prices, amounts, package values, phone numbers, QR codes, brand names, or campaign claims.
Avoid: visible source photography, collages, gouache, oil, cartoon, sticker borders, plastic 3D, slick digital illustration, dense typography, invented facts, names, logos, QR codes, watermarks, garbled text.
```

## Watercolor Visual Base For Controlled Text Overlay

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-safe vertical poster background
Primary request: Create a watercolor travel-card visual base matching the provided source poster, but keep all important text areas clean for later exact overlay.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and layout reference.
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory paper with a transparent watercolor subject and a restrained archive column, leaving clean zones for the headline and brand copy.
Subject: [describe source product props without introducing new brands].
Composition/framing: preserve the source poster's hierarchy and leave calm, low-detail paper zones for [headline], [coupon ribbon], [offer card], [QR area], and [service line].
Style/medium: transparent watercolor, fine pen lines, cold-pressed grain, paper highlights.
Lighting/mood: quiet, gentle, restrained.
Constraints: no readable generated text except simple placeholder shapes; no unrelated campaign references.
Avoid: fake logo, fake QR code, extra brands, changed offer content, gouache, heavy ink.
```

## New Poster From Copy Brief

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical campaign poster
Primary request: Design a watercolor travel-card poster from the provided approved copy brief.
Approved copy: [paste exact text].
Brand assets: [describe approved logo/brand colors].
Information hierarchy: [headline first, offer second, product scene third, action/legal last].
Style/medium: transparent watercolor travel card, perforated archive column, fine pen lines.
Composition/framing: clear text zones, central watercolor subject.
Lighting/mood: quiet, gentle, restrained.
Constraints: use only the approved copy and assets; preserve all numbers and offer terms exactly.
Avoid: invented offers, unrelated brands, unreadable small print, gouache, heavy ink.
```

## Verification Checklist

- Does the final poster still communicate the same offer?
- Are all prices, data amounts, phone numbers, and dates unchanged?
- Are brand names and logos from the current source only?
- Is the QR code copied from the source or approved asset when it needs to scan?
- Are text zones legible and not lost in the washes?
- Did the watercolor treatment change only visual style, not campaign content?
