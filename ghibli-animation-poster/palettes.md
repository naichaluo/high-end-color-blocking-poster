# Ghibli Animation Poster Palettes

Use these palettes to translate the source poster's colors into soft watercolor and pastoral light while keeping the brand's recognizable color signal. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Sunlit Meadow Green

Best for food, lifestyle, travel, home, and wholesome seasonal campaigns.

- sky: pale powder-blue, soft cream cloud
- ground: warm grass green, golden hay, ivory path
- accent: soft cornflower, warm terracotta, honey yellow
- light: glowing late-afternoon sun, dappled shadow, gentle haze
- detail: fluffy clouds, wildflower dots, distant hills, small cottage

## Warm Milk And Tea

Best for tea, bakery, dessert, gift, and member-benefit offers.

- sky: soft warm beige, blush cream
- ground: butter cream, oat, pale wheat
- accent: caramel, honeyed gold, muted berry
- light: soft morning window light, warm bounce, calm haze
- detail: linen cloth, steamed cups, gentle foliage, warm wooden tones

## Fresh Fern Garden

Best for green tea, cosmetics, natural, and wellness campaigns.

- sky: pale celadon, misty white
- ground: fern green, leaf, soft jade
- accent: apricot, cream, soft sage
- light: cool daylight with a warm accent, soft green bounce
- detail: translucent leaves, water droplets, quiet garden path, soft moss

## Calm Evening Blue

Best for premium, tech, finance-adjacent, and formal service campaigns.

- sky: cloudy slate-blue, soft dusk lavender
- ground: muted teal, cream, pale grey-green
- accent: restrained warm amber, soft white
- light: even soft overcast light, low contrast, serene mood
- detail: still water reflection, quiet rooftops, gentle mist, sparse foliage

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal.
- Keep a light, airy sky and a calm ground band so text stays readable.
- Prefer misty, translucent, watercolor transitions over hard color blocks.
- Add one warm highlight and one cool shadow to get that glowing Ghibli light.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve a calmer, lower-detail zone behind it.
