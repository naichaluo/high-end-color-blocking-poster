# Clay Animation Poster Palettes

Use palettes to translate the source poster's colors into tactile clay and miniature-set materials. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Fresh Green Membership

Best for telecom, lifestyle benefits, tea, fruit, and refreshment campaigns.

- background: matcha green, pale celadon, warm ivory
- primary clay: leaf green, cream, soft yellow, translucent grape purple
- accents: satin gold ribbon, white highlight clay, dark moss text
- lighting: soft morning backlight with gentle green bounce
- materials: polymer clay leaves, clay cups, translucent jelly clay, satin ribbon, frosted acrylic plaque

## Warm Retail Coupon

Best for food coupons, member benefits, and cheerful consumer offers.

- background: butter cream, light peach, muted coral
- primary clay: milk white, caramel, fruit orange, berry red
- accents: soft gold, cocoa brown, fresh mint
- lighting: warm tabletop studio with visible cast shadows
- materials: kneaded coupon slabs, clay coins, paper-clay tags, matte product props

## Clean Corporate Benefit

Best for telecom, service packages, finance-adjacent offers, and formal member plans.

- background: off-white, light grey-green, pale sky
- primary clay: brand color, white, muted secondary color
- accents: restrained metallic clay, clear acrylic-like clay plates
- lighting: even softbox lighting, low contrast
- materials: sculpted icon chips, rounded plan cards, clean clay dividers

## Premium Gift Exchange

Best for exchange malls, reward programs, and gift bundles.

- background: ivory, champagne, pale sage
- primary clay: cream, jade green, berry, soft gold
- accents: ribbon gold, embossed seal, pearl white
- lighting: premium tabletop lighting with soft rim highlights
- materials: clay gift boxes, satin ribbon strips, molded badge seals, raised-letter plaques

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal.
- Use at least one neutral clay color to keep text readable.
- Use metallics sparingly; make them look like satin clay or craft foil, not chrome.
- Avoid one-note color fields. Add neutral cream, brand color, product color, and one accent.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve calm, low-detail zones behind it.
