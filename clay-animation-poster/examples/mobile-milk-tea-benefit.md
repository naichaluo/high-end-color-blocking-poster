# Example: Mobile Milk Tea Benefit Poster

## Source

A vertical China Mobile poster advertising a milk tea benefit. The active source poster is the only content reference.

## Locked Content

- brand: 中国移动 / China Mobile
- headline: 移动请你喝奶茶
- main offer: 每月20元奶茶券
- subhead: 精致好物随心兑
- product scene: two drinks with fruit elements
- package line: 15元/月 享 15GB流量 + 20元饮品券
- action: QR code area with 扫码办理
- bottom service/company information

## Application

Use the Skill to create a clay-style visual base with a green handmade miniature set, two sculpted drinks, fruit props, a satin-clay ribbon, rounded offer panel, and reserved readable zones. Then overlay the locked text, QR code, and service details exactly.

## Anti-Contamination Check

Do not add unrelated tea brands, restaurant brands, KFC content, earlier coupon wording, or previous campaign visuals unless they appear in the current source poster.
