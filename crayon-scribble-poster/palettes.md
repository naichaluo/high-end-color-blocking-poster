# Crayon Scribble Poster Palettes

Use these palettes to translate the source poster's colors into bold wax-crayon strokes while keeping the brand's recognizable color signal. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Sunny Kid Energy

Best for snacks, beverages, kids, fun lifestyle, and cheerful consumer offers.

- paper: warm cream, soft kraft
- crayon: bright yellow-orange, cherry red, grass green, sky blue
- accents: white crayon highlight, dark blue outline
- stroke: bold layered scribble, generous fill, energetic
- detail: doodle sun, sparkle marks, smiley accents, star shapes

## Fresh Fruit Pop

Best for tea, juice, fruit, and refreshment campaigns.

- paper: warm off-white, pale peach
- crayon: lime green, tangerine, berry pink, mellow yellow
- accents: white highlight, dark teal outline
- stroke: overlapping scribble fill, playful brush-drawn edges
- detail: citrus slice doodle, leaf scribble, bounce marks

## Cozy Warm Candy

Best for bakery, dessert, gift, and member-benefit offers.

- paper: butter cream, oat
- crayon: caramel, berry red, honey gold, mint green
- accents: cocoa outline, white sparkle
- stroke: soft layered scribble, rounded, warm
- detail: heart doodle, ribbon scribble, tiny star dots

## Confident Bold Pop

Best for retail, promo, sale, and high-energy campaigns.

- paper: white, light grey
- crayon: true blue, crimson, mustard, black outline
- accents: white negative space, strong contrast
- stroke: thick confident scribble, high contrast, punchy
- detail: burst marks, arrows, price-tag doodles

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal.
- Keep a warm paper ground so text stays readable.
- Prefer bold overlapping scribble fill over neat fills or gradients.
- Use one strong outline color for clarity and one bright accent for energy.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve a calmer, lower-detail zone behind it.
