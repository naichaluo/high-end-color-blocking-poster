# Prompt Templates

Use these templates as starting points. Replace bracketed text with the current source content. Remove any line that does not apply.

## Existing Poster To Crayon Scribble

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: vertical commercial poster
Primary request: Redraw the provided source poster as a bold wax-crayon scribble hand-drawn poster.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and content reference.
Source-of-truth content: preserve the exact brand, headline, offer amount, product labels, QR position, service text, and overall information hierarchy from Image 1.
Style/medium: wax-crayon hand-drawn drawing, visible waxy strokes, bold layered scribble fill, slightly rough uneven crayon edges, warm paper texture, playful childlike energy.
Composition/framing: keep the source poster's main layout logic: top brand and headline, central product scene, lower offer module, QR/action area, bottom service/legal line.
Scene/backdrop: [describe one central scribble subject and a calmer background, e.g. a doodled cup on a warm cream paper ground with a crayon sun and sparkles].
Lighting/mood: bright, joyful, honest hand-made energy; warm and friendly, not corporate-hard.
Color palette: translate the source palette into bold crayon colors; keep official brand color recognizable; keep a warm paper ground for readability.
Text handling: leave quiet areas for exact text overlays; do not invent or alter business copy.
Constraints: do not mix in any content from previous projects or other reference images; do not change prices, amounts, package values, phone numbers, QR codes, brand names, or campaign claims.
Avoid: clean vector, soft watercolor, glossy CGI, photorealistic render, cold sterile look, unrelated brand names, changed coupon value, invented slogans, distorted QR code, unreadable legal text, cluttered doodles that bury the subject.
```

## Crayon Visual Base For Controlled Text Overlay

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-safe vertical poster background
Primary request: Create a crayon-scribble visual base matching the provided source poster, but keep all important text areas clean for later exact overlay.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and layout reference.
Scene/backdrop: warm paper ground with a bold crayon subject and accent doodles inspired by the source.
Subject: [describe source product props without introducing new brands].
Composition/framing: preserve the source poster's hierarchy and leave calm, lower-detail zones for [headline], [coupon ribbon], [offer card], [QR area], and [service line].
Style/medium: wax-crayon scribble, bold layered strokes, warm paper grain, playful energy.
Lighting/mood: bright and joyful, clean commercial readability.
Constraints: no readable generated text except simple placeholder shapes; no unrelated campaign references.
Avoid: fake logo, fake QR code, extra brands, changed offer content, neat vector fill.
```

## New Poster From Copy Brief

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical campaign poster
Primary request: Design a crayon-scribble poster from the provided approved copy brief.
Approved copy: [paste exact text].
Brand assets: [describe approved logo/brand colors].
Information hierarchy: [headline first, offer second, product scene third, action/legal last].
Style/medium: wax-crayon hand-drawn, bold scribble, playful energy, warm paper.
Composition/framing: clear text zones, central scribble product scene, calmer background.
Lighting/mood: bright and joyful, warm commercial mood.
Constraints: use only the approved copy and assets; preserve all numbers and offer terms exactly.
Avoid: invented offers, unrelated brands, unreadable small print, neat vector fill.
```

## Verification Checklist

- Does the final poster still communicate the same offer?
- Are all prices, data amounts, phone numbers, and dates unchanged?
- Are brand names and logos from the current source only?
- Is the QR code copied from the source or approved asset when it needs to scan?
- Are text zones legible and not lost in the scribbles?
- Did the crayon treatment change only visual style, not campaign content?
