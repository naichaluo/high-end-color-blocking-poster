# Paper Collage Poster Method

## Visual grammar

- Material: matte colored paper, recycled stock, newspaper, vellum, card, or screen-printed sheets.
- Edges: clean die-cut for product silhouettes; torn/deckled edges for atmosphere; folded edges for directional movement.
- Depth: use 3–5 meaningful layers rather than dozens of decorative scraps.
- Shadows: one consistent light direction, short soft cast shadows, slightly imperfect registration.
- Texture: subtle fibers, ink grain, halftone, and edge wear; never let texture reduce copy legibility.
- Composition: one dominant object, one supporting metaphor, one headline zone, one CTA zone.

## Layer map template

1. Paper field: the full-canvas stock and quiet texture.
2. Spatial cue: map, horizon, room, road, cloud, wave, or geometric environment.
3. Hero subject: the product, person silhouette, device, package, or central symbol.
4. Functional cutouts: icons, cards, tools, leaves, signal lines, or supporting objects.
5. Accent layer: one tactile detail that gives the metaphor a memorable beat.
6. Overlay-safe zone: reserved blank paper for exact title, offer, logo, QR, or legal copy.

## Prompt skeleton

```text
Transform the supplied ordinary poster into a premium paper-cut collage advertising key visual.
Preserve the campaign subject and visual priority, but rebuild the scene from layered matte paper,
die-cut and torn edges, tactile paper fibers, screen-printed ink, subtle halftone, and coherent
soft cast shadows. Use one clear visual metaphor: [METAPHOR]. Composition: [FOCAL SUBJECT],
[SUPPORTING ELEMENTS], [HEADLINE ZONE], [CTA ZONE]. Keep the exact text, logo, URL, price,
date, and QR code out of the generated artwork; reserve clean paper space for later overlay.
Editorial advertising finish, tactile but controlled, readable at thumbnail size, no unrelated scraps.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
photorealistic product shot, glossy 3D render, plastic CGI, chrome, neon cyberpunk,
generic scrapbook, chaotic paper scraps, crowded composition, random extra objects,
fake logo, misspelled brand, unreadable Chinese text, incorrect numbers, invented QR code,
watermark, duplicate subject, inconsistent shadows, muddy layers, excessive texture,
low-contrast headline area, childish craft look
```

## Deliverable variants

- Concept-only: angle, metaphor, layer map, composition, and copy-safe prompt.
- Key-visual prompt pack: main prompt, negative prompt, overlay plan, and 2 restrained variants.
- Generated poster: image-model output plus exact post-production overlay checklist.
- Motion extension: 5–8 second assembly storyboard with entry direction, settle, hold, and text-overlay timing.
