# Prompt Templates

Use these templates as starting points. Replace bracketed text with the current source content. Remove any line that does not apply.

## Existing Poster To Fabric Patchwork

```text
Use case: style-transfer
Asset type: vertical commercial poster
Primary request: Redraw the provided source poster as a fabric patchwork / hand-sewn fabric collage.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and content reference.
Source-of-truth content: preserve the exact brand, headline, offer amount, product labels, QR position, service text, and overall information hierarchy from Image 1.
Style/medium: layered patchwork of different fabrics — linen, canvas, gingham/check, floral print, plain cotton, denim, felt — raw frayed edges with visible loose thread at cut borders, visible hand-running-stitch seams with uneven spacing tracing patch edges, fabric buttons (round/square with thread cross-holes) and small cloth shapes (stars, hearts, bows, string/ribbon accents), matte tactile woven surface, soft natural light, gentle cast shadows, warm zako/Japanese-craft everyday-heartwarming mood.
Composition/framing: keep the source poster's main layout logic: top brand and headline, central product scene, lower offer module, QR/action area, bottom service/legal line.
Scene/backdrop: the background is ONE single continuous piece of gingham/check cloth (default) covering the FULL canvas edge-to-edge, unbroken, with no exposed other substrate and no blank margin; place decorative patches only around the subject, not across the whole ground.
Lighting/mood: warm soft natural light, cozy hand-made mood.
Color palette: translate the source palette into cloth colors; keep official brand color recognizable; keep a neutral base cloth for readability.
Text handling: leave quiet areas for exact text overlays; do not invent or alter business copy.
Constraints: do not mix in any content from previous projects or other reference images; do not change prices, amounts, package values, phone numbers, QR codes, brand names, or campaign claims.
Avoid: paper collage, torn-paper, digital texture filter, glossy plastic, photorealistic render, cold sterile look, unrelated brand names, changed coupon value, invented slogans, distorted QR code, unreadable legal text, cloth scraps that bury the subject.
```

## Fabric Patchwork Visual Base For Controlled Text Overlay

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-safe vertical poster background
Primary request: Create a fabric patchwork visual base matching the provided source poster, but keep all important text areas clean for later exact overlay.
Input image: Image 1 is the only visual and layout reference.
Scene/backdrop: warm cloth field with layered patchwork patches, a central fabric product prop, and stitched accents inspired by the source.
Subject: [describe source product props without introducing new brands].
Composition/framing: preserve the source poster's hierarchy and leave calm, lower-detail fabric zones for [headline], [coupon ribbon], [offer card], [QR area], and [service line].
Style/medium: hand-sewn fabric patchwork, frayed edges, running stitches, buttons, matte woven texture.
Lighting/mood: warm soft natural light, cozy hand-made readability.
Constraints: no readable generated text except simple placeholder shapes; no unrelated campaign references.
Avoid: fake logo, fake QR code, extra brands, changed offer content, paper collage, glossy gloss.
```

## New Poster From Copy Brief

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical campaign poster
Primary request: Design a fabric patchwork poster from the provided approved copy brief.
Approved copy: [paste exact text].
Brand assets: [describe approved logo/brand colors].
Information hierarchy: [headline first, offer second, product scene third, action/legal last].
Style/medium: hand-sewn fabric patchwork, layered cloth, frayed edges, stitches, buttons.
Composition/framing: clear text zones, central fabric product scene, calmer cloth background.
Lighting/mood: warm soft natural light, cozy hand-made mood.
Constraints: use only the approved copy and assets; preserve all numbers and offer terms exactly.
Avoid: invented offers, unrelated brands, unreadable small print, paper collage, glossy render.
```

## Verification Checklist

- Does the final poster still communicate the same offer?
- Are all prices, data amounts, phone numbers, and dates unchanged?
- Are brand names and logos from the current source only?
- Is the QR code copied from the source or approved asset when it needs to scan?
- Are text zones legible and not lost in the cloth patches?
- Does it read as a designed fabric patchwork, not a paper collage or filter?
- Did the fabric treatment change only visual style, not campaign content?
