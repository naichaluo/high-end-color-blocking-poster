# RISO Print Redesign Poster Palettes

Use these limited-ink palettes to translate the source poster's colors into 2-3 RISO inks plus neutral paper while keeping the brand's recognizable color signal. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Rust Coral And Ink

Best for warm lifestyle, food, travel, and welcoming consumer campaigns.

- paper: neutral natural-white
- ink: rust coral (dominant), deep ink blue, warm cream
- secondary: ink black at low coverage
- mood: confident, social, warm
- detail: hard contour wobble, unequal junction gaps, paper slivers

## Fresh Green And Peach

Best for tea, fruit, natural, and wellness campaigns.

- paper: neutral natural-white
- ink: leaf green (dominant), peach, soft cream
- secondary: deep green, minimal black
- mood: fresh, airy, organic
- detail: clean large fields, registration offset, sparse halftone accents

## Warm Red And Mustard

Best for retail, promo, sale, and high-energy campaigns.

- paper: neutral natural-white
- ink: bright red (dominant), mustard yellow, ink black
- secondary: black for hierarchy at ≤25%
- mood: punchy, editorial, bold
- detail: strong silhouette, short ink drags, missing-ink rubs

## Calm Blue And Gold

Best for premium, tech, finance-adjacent, and formal service campaigns.

- paper: neutral natural-white
- ink: slate blue (dominant), muted gold, cream
- secondary: restrained ink black
- mood: calm, precise, elegant
- detail: crisp edges, uneven gaps, slight overprint, controlled ink density

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal, but express it as ink not photo color.
- Use 2-3 inks plus neutral natural-white paper; keep black secondary (≤25%).
- Keep large fields clean; name only a few local zones for halftone or ink density marks.
- Preserve the palette's saturation and contrast as ink—vivid anchors keep a high-chroma clash, restrained anchors stay clear through distinct mass boundaries.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve a calmer, lower-detail ink zone behind it.
