# Plush Animation Poster Palettes

Use these palettes to translate the source poster's colors into soft felt, fleece, and yarn materials while keeping the brand's recognizable color signal. Do not override official brand colors when they are content-critical.

## Cozy Village Green

Best for food, leisure, lifestyle, travel, and wholesome seasonal campaigns.

- backdrop: moss green felt, warm ivory fleece, soft cream
- primary plush: sage green, toffee brown, milk white, brick red
- accents: dusty blue, mustard yarn, pom-pom cream
- lighting: soft morning window light, gentle warm bounce
- materials: felted hills, yarn pom-poms, fleece props, button details

## Warm Milk-Tea Cream

Best for tea, bakery, dessert, gift, and member-benefit offers.

- backdrop: butter cream, pale peach, soft oat
- primary plush: milk white, caramel, fruit pink, honey
- accents: soft cocoa, mint green, ribbon cream
- lighting: warm tabletop mini-set with soft cast shadows
- materials: knitted mugs, felt cakes, fleece berries, stitch details

## Fresh Fern Garden

Best for green tea, cosmetics, natural, and wellness campaigns.

- backdrop: pale celadon, fern green, warm paper white
- primary plush: leaf green, cream, soft jade, pale apricot
- accents: satin-gold felt, translucent gel accents, moss detail
- lighting: soft daylight with gentle green bounce
- materials: felt leaves, fleece petals, yarn stems, stitch leaf veins

## Calm Evening Blue

Best for premium, tech, finance-adjacent, and formal service campaigns.

- backdrop: off-white, dust blue, soft slate
- primary plush: muted navy, cream, pale slate-blue
- accents: restrained metallic felt, clear acrylic-like accents
- lighting: even softbox lighting, low contrast, calm mood
- materials: sculpted icon chips, rounded plush cards, clean felt dividers

## Palette Rules

- Keep the source brand's recognizable color signal.
- Use at least one neutral warm (cream/ivory) so text stays readable.
- Keep the palette quiet and natural; plush is cozy, not neon.
- Add one accent fiber color and one metal/stitch accent for depth.
- If text is overlaid later, reserve calm, low-detail zones behind it.
