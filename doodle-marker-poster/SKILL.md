# Doodle Marker Poster / 涂鸦马克笔手绘海报

把普通海报、产品描述或营销主视觉，重绘成 **手绘涂鸦 / 稚拙插画 / 马克笔手绘**风广告。

**整体追求**：稚拙手绘 × 成熟构图 × 大量留白 × 聪明图文关系 × 独立出版物气质。

**只改变视觉语言，不改变商业内容。**

This skill is independent from every other poster skill. Do not merge or append another skill's rules unless the user explicitly requests a combined treatment.

## Trigger conditions

Use this skill when the user asks to:

- 把普通海报改成手绘涂鸦风、稚拙插画风、马克笔手绘风、蜡笔/油画棒手绘风；
- create a Hand-drawn Doodle Lifestyle Illustration / Naive Illustration / Marker Drawing poster;
- rebuild a key visual with loose hand-drawn lines and a large warm-white negative field;
- give a campaign visual an independent-publication (独立出版物) feel.

Do not use it for watercolor, gouache, pixel, plush, clay, 3D, or photorealistic tasks with no hand-drawn transformation.

## Non-negotiable decisions

1. **Separate locked communication from visual reinterpretation.** Lock the product name, offer, price, dates, URL, QR code, legal copy, brand colors when supplied, and the user's approved wording. Reinterpret line quality, shape language, arrangement, and type placement.
2. **Re-extract the subject.** 重新提炼源海报中最具识别性的主体、轮廓、动作、结构与叙事关系；**删去大部分无关背景和细节**。
3. **Build a readable structure**: 大面积暖白/浅色留白作为场 → 小型到中型主体 → 手绘符号 → 与图形同系统的文字。
4. **Treat Chinese text, numbers, URLs, logos, and QR codes as production overlays.** Do not ask the image model to redraw them. Leave clean reserved areas and add exact assets later in a deterministic editor.
5. **The poster must still work at thumbnail size.** 主体必须一眼可辨。

## Style definition

Hand-drawn doodle / naive illustration / marker drawing — a designed independent-publication page, not a children's notebook page.

- **线条**：松弛、略带抖动和断续感的手绘线条；线条有轻重变化与手工误差，可重复描、可断开。
- **上色**：马克笔、蜡笔、油画棒般的自然平涂；色块边缘略有溢出或没涂满，保留手工误差，不做精细渐变。
- **造型**：简化、符号化、略带夸张；**主体一眼可辨**；不追求写实比例、复杂透视和精细描绘。
- **构图**：下半部分以大面积暖白或浅色留白为核心；主体只占较小到适中的视觉面积；主体可偏心、贴边、悬置、裁切或局部越界；通过尺度变化、错位和不对称关系建立节奏与呼吸感。
- **图文关系**：文字与图形必须作为**同一构图系统**设计。文字采用轻巧自然的手写字或简洁编辑字体，可沿主体轮廓、动作方向或留白区域排布；**避免机械居中和上下堆叠**。
- **配色**：**主体的色彩必须保留源海报的主要色彩信息**——产品、包装、品牌色与核心人物的实际颜色忠实保留，不因风格化而改变色相或明度关系。**2–5 色收敛与大面积浅色场只作用于背景/留白场与次要装饰，不作用于主体。** 整体仍柔和、明快、清爽、治愈，鲜活但不过饱和。
- **气质**：稚拙手绘 × 成熟构图 × 大量留白 × 聪明图文关系 × 独立出版物气质。

## Composition rules

- **画布保留源海报的原始尺寸与宽高比**（按模型允许规整：宽高为 16 的倍数、像素积达标，尽量贴近原尺寸）。
- **保留源海报的信息顺序与层级关系**（品牌/标题 → 主体场景 → 卖点/优惠 → QR/行动区 → 服务/法务），但**允许按本风格的松弛不对称构图重新排布**。
- **不得为了"留白美学"删减、弱化或省略源海报的必需信息**。留白是靠**布局**腾出来的，不是靠删信息换来的。
- 留出大面积、可呼吸的暖白/浅色区域，让主体与文字都轻巧地落在其中。
- 避免复杂背景与信息堆积；避免随机小图标填充画面。

## Text and brand handling

For commercial posters, model-generated text is risky. Prefer a two-layer workflow:

- **visual layer:** 主体涂鸦、手绘符号、留白场、装饰性笔触
- **controlled layer:** exact headline, offer amount, brand name, QR code, phone number, address/company name, disclaimers, and product names

**文字规则**：

- **逐字保留**源海报的标题与全部文案；价格、券额、日期、电话、URL、二维码、品牌名一律不改。
- **不新增、不编造**标题或文案；本风格不另拟标题——源海报的原有标题就是本构图的标题元素。
- 文字的"轻巧排布"体现在**字体与位置**（手写字或简洁编辑字体、沿轮廓/动作方向/留白排布、避免机械居中与上下堆叠），**不体现在删字**。
- 不得把文字或 logo 化为不可读色块。
- 若 logo 或二维码必须可用，从源海报或品牌素材直接贴回，不要让模型自己画。

When using image generation, quote required text verbatim in the prompt, but still verify and correct it afterward.

## Workflow

1. Identify the active source of truth: source poster, text brief, brand kit, or user-provided copy.
2. Extract locked content before styling: brand name/logo, headline, offer amount, product labels, QR code, legal/service text, and visible hierarchy.
3. Re-extract the recognisable subject, silhouette, action, structure, and narrative relation; decide what background and detail to drop.
4. Choose a treatment from `palettes.md` and a prompt pattern from `prompt-templates.md`.
5. If exact text matters, generate only the hand-drawn visual layer and plan a text overlay.
6. Reapply exact text, QR codes, logos, prices, and service details as controlled overlay layers when possible.
7. Review the result against the source and remove any content that came from another project.

## Current-project isolation

Before final delivery, perform a contamination check:

- no brand names from unrelated projects
- no product names from unrelated posters
- no slogans, mascots, coupons, or visual motifs copied from previous turns unless they are in the current source
- no changed offer amounts
- no replaced QR code or phone number unless the user explicitly asks

## Supporting files

- `palettes.md`: extracted 2–5 colour palettes and material treatments.
- `prompt-templates.md`: reusable prompts for style transfer and poster generation.
- `examples/`: example briefs and prompt applications.

## Quality gate

- Does the canvas keep the source poster's original dimensions and aspect ratio?
- Is there a **large warm-white / pale field**, with the subject occupying only a small-to-moderate visual area?
- Is the subject **off-centre / edge-touching / suspended / cropped / partially out of bounds**, with rhythm built from scale change, displacement, and asymmetry?
- Are the lines loose, slightly wobbly, and broken, with handmade error?
- Are the fills marker / crayon / oil-pastel flat, with slight overspill or gaps, not graded?
- Is the subject simplified, symbolic, slightly exaggerated, yet **instantly recognisable**?
- **Are the main subject's colours faithful to the source poster's principal colour information** — product, packaging, brand colour, and key figures keep their actual hues, not stylised away?
- Is the **background / decorative** palette reduced to **2–5 lively source-derived colours** on a large pale field — soft / bright / fresh / healing, not oversaturated?
- Are text and graphics designed as **one composition system**, with type along contours, action direction, or the field — **not** mechanically centred or stacked top-and-bottom?
- **Are all source text, prices, offer amounts, QR code, and logos preserved exactly and readable?**
- Is the result free of complex backgrounds, information clutter, random small icons, cheap cartoon feel, children's-notebook feel, 3D, and template layout?
