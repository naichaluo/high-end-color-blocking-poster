# Torn-Paper Relief Method

## Visual signature

- Warm ivory, pale sage, or uncoated off-white paper field.
- Broad torn-paper bands, irregular deckled edges, curled corners, and a recessed product window.
- Visible paper thickness, contact shadows, shallow relief, and soft studio light.
- Low-saturation natural green, cream, ochre, apricot, plum, or muted berry, with dark olive/brown copy.
- Centered hero product cluster on stepped paper plinths with a few ingredient or benefit cues.
- Clean upper headline zone, compact offer ribbon/card, and stable lower information band.

## Layer recipe

1. Background paper field with restrained grain.
2. Torn upper or middle sheet creating the reveal.
3. Recessed scenic panel in a darker muted tone.
4. Hero product or product pair with tactile surfaces.
5. Ingredients, leaves, mascot, or benefit symbols as secondary cutouts.
6. Raised offer card or ribbon.
7. Exact typography, logo, QR, and legal overlays added after generation.

## Prompt vocabulary

Use concrete terms: premium torn-paper advertising set, uncoated paper, deckled fiber edge, layered paper relief, shallow bas-relief, tactile matte stock, folded paper ribbon, recessed paper window, soft contact shadow, editorial product staging, quiet natural palette, clean copy-safe area.

Avoid vague “beautiful collage” wording. Explain where torn paper is, what it reveals, which object is focal, and where exact copy must remain clear.

## Failure prevention

- Remove invented Chinese text and reserve the area for typesetting.
- If too craft-like, reduce scraps and emphasize one product stage, studio light, and disciplined grid.
- If product identity weakens, lock its silhouette, packaging geometry, color family, and supplied mascot/logo.
- If flat, add thickness, overlap, recessed depth, and contact shadows rather than decoration.
- If noisy, keep field and cards near ivory/sage and reserve stronger color for product accents.
